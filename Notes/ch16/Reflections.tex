\section*{General Thoughts}
Difference in differences is a classic technique, so brushing up on it is always good.
Robust adjustment for covariates is a pretty nice extension. While I think that the chapter does contribute 
quite a bit, it feels a bit hollow since it covers a classic technique and the new contributions are 
pretty trivial to roll in. In many ways this is another reference chapter.

\section*{Space for future reading}
The mariel boatlift example is worth reading up on a bit more if I have extra time, just to look at how you can 
justify parallel trends and pull comparison data.
